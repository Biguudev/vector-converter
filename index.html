This export follows the contour-based structure from the provided reference, but importing successfully is not guaranteed. Test it in Alight Motion using a duplicate project first. SVG is the safer interchange format.

What this converter does / does not do
  • Traces image regions into vector paths, with one editable path/layer per detected path.
  • Can export SVG and a best-effort Alight Motion XML draft.
  • Splits by visual regions and colors, not by meaning. It will not automatically know that a part is hair, a face, a shirt, or a body part.
  • It is not AI background removal. Best results are usually flat illustrations, cartoons, icons and logos; detailed photos can create a very large number of paths.
  • To keep processing manageable, large images are downscaled to a maximum side length of 1000 pixels before tracing.
